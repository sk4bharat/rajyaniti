✦ Writing Guidelines

  • Ground every claim in primary sources or peer-reviewed archaeology.
  • Prefer specific dates, sites, and artefacts over generalisation.
  • Challenge popular myths — but do so with evidence, not assertion.
  • Aim for at least 800 words; in-depth essays work best at 1,500–3,000.
  • Acknowledge scholarly disagreement where it genuinely exists.
  • Avoid anachronism — interpret the past on its own terms.
