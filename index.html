✦ About

Writing India
from the inside

sk4bharat writes at the crossroads of mythology, history, culture, and contemporary Indian life. The work draws on a belief that to understand where India is going, one must first understand — without romanticizing — where it has been.

Articles, books, and talks have appeared across national publications, universities, and literary festivals. The audience spans students of history, professionals navigating modern India, and anyone who suspects that the old stories still have something urgent to say.
